Built around
the timer

Delts treats the red start button as the source of truth. Add workouts, start the timer, log the work, and keep the rest quiet.

01

Workout timer

The red button starts and ends the session, drives all timing, and runs a Live Activity on your Lock Screen.

TIMER
02

Set, rep & RPE logging

Log the real work inline while the timer runs — strength RPE or CR10, your call.

LOG
03

AI Coach

Chat with a coach that sees your real workouts, progress, and goals — attach photos of equipment, meals, or form.

PREMIUM
04

Calorie burn estimates

Automatic kcal after every session from your workout and body data, synced to Apple Health.

PREMIUM
05

Exercise library

845 exercises with photos and form instructions, filtered by split, muscle, and equipment.
