Privacy
Policy

01

Overview

Delts is a free iPhone exercise library. No account is required and the app works entirely on your device. Delts does not collect, sell, or share any personal information.

02

What stays on your device

Your preferences — the color theme, light/dark appearance, and the exercise filters and sort you choose — are stored locally on your iPhone using the standard iOS settings store. That information never leaves your device, and deleting the app removes it.

03

No accounts, ads, or tracking

There is no sign-up or login, no advertising, no in-app analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs. Delts does not build a profile of you or follow you across apps or websites.

04

App update check

To tell you when a newer version is available, Delts asks Apple's public App Store lookup service whether a higher version of the app exists. This request sends only the app's bundle identifier — no personal data, and nothing that identifies you.

05

No permissions requested

Delts does not request access to Apple Health, your camera, photos, location, contacts, or notifications. The exercise content is bundled inside the app.

06

Website analytics

This website (delts.fit) uses privacy-friendly, aggregate web analytics to count visits. It does not use cross-site tracking cookies. This applies to the website only, not the app.

07

Your choices and deletion

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to export or request. Deleting the app removes all of its local preferences from your device.

Contact

09

Changes

This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. The date above reflects the latest published version.
