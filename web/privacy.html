Privacy
Policy

01

Overview

Delts is an iPhone workout planning and tracking app. No account is required, and the core app works entirely on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles, what leaves your device when you use the AI features, and the choices you have.

02

What stays on your device

Your workout plans, logged sets, reps and RPE, workout history, body weight and body fat logs, profile details (age, sex, height, weight, goals, equipment), app settings, and your Coach chat history are stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes this data.

03

AI features (Premium)

When you use the AI Coach or automatic calorie estimates, the data needed to answer is sent to our server (delts.fit) and processed by Google's Gemini API: your Coach messages, any photos you choose to attach, and training context such as your profile basics, goals, recent workouts, and body progress. Calorie estimates send the finished session and your body data. Responses are returned to your device; we do not sell this data or use it for advertising. Google processes requests under its API terms. If you never use the AI features, none of this data leaves your phone.

04

Purchases and subscriptions

Delts Premium is billed by Apple through your App Store account — we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage subscription status, identified by an anonymous identifier and purchase receipt data, not your name or email.

05

Device identifier

AI requests include an anonymous per-install device identifier used only for abuse prevention and rate limiting. It is not linked to your identity.

06

Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional. If you grant permission, Delts can read and write body weight and body fat records, and save completed workouts with estimated calories. You can revoke Health access in iOS Settings at any time.

07

Website analytics

This website (delts.fit) uses privacy-friendly, aggregate web analytics to count visits. It does not use cross-site tracking cookies.

08

Your choices and deletion

Use the app without AI and nothing leaves your device. Delete the app to remove all local data. Manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings. For anything else, contact us and we will help.

Changes
